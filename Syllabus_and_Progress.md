## 🎯 Course Goals
- Master Unix/Linux shell for managing genomic data.
- Learn standardized formats: FASTQ, BED, GTF, SAM/BAM.
- Use bioinformatics tools: Samtools, Bedtools, Bowtie, Bcftools.
